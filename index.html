Privacy Policy

Steady — Sober Days
Last updated: September 1, 2026

Carey Pillar ("we", "us") built Steady as a free, private cannabis sobriety tracker and daily mental health practice. This policy describes what data the app handles and how.

Data Collection

Steady collects zero data. No accounts. No email. No analytics. No crash reporting. No advertising. No third-party SDKs. There is no developer-accessible backend. We cannot see, access, or retrieve any of your data.

Data Stored on Your Device

Steady stores the following data locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Quit date and sobriety streak
  • Daily check-ins (an emotion word, sleep hours, a short reflection, dreams; older entries may hold mood/energy/anxiety ratings)
  • Craving logs (trigger, intensity, duration)
  • Slip records (timestamp, trigger, private notes)
  • Reaction-time test results
  • Achievement unlocks
  • Weekly reflections (three good things)
  • Calm tool usage records
  • "Good I did" entries (short notes and a category)
  • Letters you write to your future self
  • Early Signals check-ins (your own yes/no items and two optional short questionnaire scores), if you turn that feature on
  • Your plan card, if you fill one in — including the names and phone numbers of people you choose to list. Those contacts are stored only in your own iCloud with the rest of your data and are never sent anywhere else.
  • Cost/time baselines you enter

iCloud Sync

If you are signed into iCloud, your data syncs to your private iCloud container via Apple CloudKit. This syncs data across your own devices only. Steady uses CloudKit's private database exclusively. Your iCloud data is protected by your Apple ID and Apple's iCloud security infrastructure. We have no access to your iCloud container.

Apple HealthKit

With your explicit permission (toggled in Settings), Steady reads the following from Apple Health:

  • Sleep analysis (asleep core, deep, REM durations)
  • Heart rate variability (HRV SDNN)
  • Exercise time

Steady never writes to HealthKit. Health data is read on-device, computed in memory, and never stored in SwiftData or synced to CloudKit. Only the fact of computed achievement unlocks (e.g., "First Full Night") is stored. Raw HealthKit data never leaves your device's HealthKit sandbox.

Optional Partner Sharing

You may optionally share milestone and slip events with up to three trusted people via Apple's CloudKit Share. The partner sees an event log only. They never see your daily data, mood, journal entries, dream logs, notes, triggers, or HealthKit data. Partner sharing is off by default and requires explicit setup.

Third-Party Services

Steady uses zero third-party services. The only Apple frameworks used are:

  • Apple CloudKit — for iCloud sync (your private container only)
  • Apple HealthKit — read-only, with your explicit permission
  • Apple UserNotifications — optional local reminders you turn on in Settings. If you use partner sharing, the app also registers for silent iCloud push so a shared event can reach your supporters; that push carries no content beyond "an update from someone you're supporting."

No analytics SDKs (no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude). No advertising SDKs. No crash reporting SDKs (no Crashlytics, no Sentry). No attribution SDKs.

Data Sharing

Steady does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. You may choose to export your data as a JSON file via the share sheet in Settings. Exported files contain all your tracker data. You control who receives exported files.

Data Deletion

Settings → “Delete all my data” removes everything Steady has stored — on the device and in your private iCloud — and cannot be undone. “Export My Data” gives you a JSON copy of every record first if you want one.

Children's Privacy

Steady is rated 17+ due to cannabis cessation content. It is not intended for children under 17.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version here with an updated date. Steady has no way to notify you of policy changes (no email, no push) — check this page if you want to verify.
